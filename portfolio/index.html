BIO

I'm Dominik, a software engineer from Rzeszów.

I've been building web applications since 2017, with the strongest focus on the frontend: e-commerce at a digital agency, D3.js dashboards at a software house, and most recently enterprise SaaS in the Salesforce ecosystem at Clari/Salesloft. I trained as a logistics engineer, which probably explains the maps.

I work with an AI-augmented workflow every day, using Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and Google Antigravity to move faster on scaffolding, refactors and tests, while I stay responsible for design decisions and code review.

I'm growing towards fullstack. I'm learning backend development in Node.js through my own products, and in earlier projects I also shipped backend fixes in Java, Ruby and Kotlin.

Outside client work I build and ship my own products end to end. Wózkowskaz is the one currently live.

Line 01LiveAug 2026 – present

Wózkowskaz

A free map for parents that checks whether a route works with a stroller. It shows the surface along the way (asphalt, cobblestones, packed earth, sand), flags stairs, high curbs and steep slopes, and marks benches, shelters, toilets and playgrounds.

Built mobile-first as a PWA, with Polish and English interface, for use on a walk.

Built with: Next.js, TypeScript, MapLibre GL, OpenStreetMap
